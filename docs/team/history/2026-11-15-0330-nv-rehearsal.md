# NV: full demo rehearsal test (2026-11-15 03:30)

Owner item 3. **Run it after every merge, NV's and Dell's:** `python -m unittest integration.tests.test_rehearsal` (about 25 s; it's also part of the full suite).

## What it is

`integration/tests/test_rehearsal.py` runs the whole demo end to end. It uses the real wiring (`integration/__main__.build_app`) and Dell's real pipeline (modes, rules, the brain offline, the log, the dashboard). Only the hardware is replaced, through `build_app(..., parts=...)`:
- the fake Flipper (the real driver over a fake serial port)
- a camera capture serving a sample door photo (the real CameraFeed, PhotoCamera, object detector and alarm recording), with vision answered by a fake LM Studio
- a live fake mic (the real Recorder, Voice, wake phrase and windows) that says each line as a tone of its own pitch, and an STT that knows the lines by pitch
- a speaker that records what Faraday says (the real mute-while-talking gate)

## The demo it walks through

1. Start: every lamp off.
2. "Hey Faraday, I'm heading out." arms Away.
3. Doorbell: fixed greeting, photo described, the visitor's window opens, and the visitor's answer is replied to.
4. Walk-up then ring: no warning, and the window still opens after a ring that gets no greeting.
5. Motion: a warning after the grace period. Motion again: the alarm, with both lights, LED red plus buzz, the alarm line and a recording saved.
6. "Hey Faraday, <passphrase>": disarmed, lights off, LED off. The passphrase never reaches the log, and that line has channel owner.
7. "Hey Faraday, what happened while I was out?": the summary is spoken.
8. "Hey Faraday, who's at the door?": a fresh photo, and the description is spoken.
9. **The seams:** every log entry validates, no part logged an ERROR, no thread crashed, nothing printed "pipeline failed", and the dashboard's `/api/state` answers.

It ran 3 times in a row without a failure. `FARADAY_REHEARSAL_DUMP=<file>` writes the whole console to a file for debugging.

## Integration bugs it found (fixed in this PR)

- **Repeat ring:** since Dell's doorbell flow (#49), a repeat ring gets no greeting. The visitor's window then waited for the 10 s fallback, and a visitor who spoke at once went into wake listening and was dropped. Now the window opens about 2 s after the ring once Faraday is idle (a greeting still comes first when there is one). `integration/voice.py`.
- **Wake phrase in a visitor window:** "Hey Faraday, <passphrase>" said while a visitor window was open came out as *visitor* speech with the wake phrase still in it, so it went to Dell's visitor conversation. A window transcript that starts with the wake phrase is now an owner request; the phrase alone asks for an owner window.
- **Stopping:** a `ControlsServer` that was never started hung `stop()`. Fixed in #56, found here first.
- **Console output:** the voice printed to stdout directly. It now goes through the app's printer (#56).

`build_app` got its `parts` hook in #56 (the real-product PR).
