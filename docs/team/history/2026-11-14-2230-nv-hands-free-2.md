# NV: hands-free 2 (2026-11-14 22:30)

Owner items 3, 4, 5 and 9 from the Mac full-system test. Built on Windows with stand-ins; the Mac confirms.

## What changed

- **One mic stream, no gaps** (`hardware/audio.py`, `Recorder`). The stream opens on the first window and stays open until the app stops. Speech is cut into back-to-back segments at pauses. The last 0.5 s before a window is kept, so a word spoken just before the window opens isn't lost. Audio is dropped while the gate is muted.
- **Listening windows** (`integration/voice.py`):
  - A window opens only when Faraday has nothing left to say (`App.speech_busy()`: no event being handled, no line queued or being spoken, no photo in progress whose description will be spoken) **and** the speaker has been quiet for `after_speech_s` (0.3 s).
  - `max_s` 15, `silence_s` 1.8, `no_speech_s` 8 (were 6, 0.8, 3). The doorbell fallback (window with no greeting) is 10 s now, because the 9B greeting takes a while.
- **Wake phrase**, continuous. Every stretch of speech while idle is transcribed. "Hey Faraday, turn on the light" in one breath is sent at once. "Hey Faraday" alone plays a short generated chime (`hardware.audio.play_chime`, two soft notes, mic muted meanwhile) and the segment that's already recording becomes the request window, so words that follow right away are kept whole. Plain "Faraday" is dropped and its audio deleted.
- **`speech_heard.channel`** (`shared/events.py` `SpeechChannel`, FORMATS.md, PLAN Q29): `"owner"` after the wake phrase or answering Faraday's question within 30 s of an owner request; `"visitor"` from a doorbell/question window; `null` for typed text. Validated.
- **Listen hook removed.** `integration/__main__.py` no longer sets `board.listen_hook`/`talk_hook`; `integration/controls.py` lost `/api/listen` and the page's Listen button. `listening_hook` stays for the mic indicator. Dell removes the dashboard button.

## Settings

In `config/settings.json` on the Mac, replace the two lines under `"audio"`:

```json
    "listen": {"max_s": 15, "silence_s": 1.8, "no_speech_s": 8, "after_speech_s": 0.3},
    "wake": {"enabled": true, "phrase": "hey faraday", "silence_s": 0.7, "max_s": 10}
```

(`window_s` is gone. The full file is in `2026-11-14-1900-nv-mac-settings.md`.)
