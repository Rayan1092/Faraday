# Dell: "I can't see the door" only answers the question (2026-11-15 01:05)

Owner's list after the Mac test, item 7. "I can't see the door right now" was spoken after a motion at Home.

**Likely cause:** the "who's at the door?" safety timer (25 s) ran out while the Mac's vision was still working. NV's vision now has a 60 s timeout and retries. The timer's line then landed after an unrelated motion.

**Fixes** (`brain/pipeline.py`, `brain/look.py`):
- The timer is now 65 s, longer than NV's vision timeout. The answer normally comes from the photo itself: described, described by the detector, or failed.
- When the timer does fire, it stays quiet if any motion, doorbell or speech came in after the question. The late photo is still dropped (no visitor greeting for it).
- `photo_failed` uses NV's `described_by` (#43). "detector" (vision failed but the object detector described the scene) is spoken, not "I can't see". No description at all (source `camera`, "Photo saved.") is still a failure.

**Tests:** `brain/tests/test_look.py`: quiet after something else happened, the timeout is longer than vision, a detector description is spoken.
