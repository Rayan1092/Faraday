# Dell: "I can't see the door" only answers the question (2026-11-15 01:05)

Owner's list after the Mac test, item 7. "I can't see the door right now" was spoken after a motion at Home.

**Possible causes** (not confirmed):
- The "who's at the door?" safety timer (25 s) ran out while a photo was still on its way, and its line landed after an unrelated motion.
- A failed photo ("Photo saved.", source `camera`) answered the question correctly, but after a motion.

**To check on the Mac:** look at the speak entry's `caused_by` in `logs/events.jsonl`. `act_...` means the timer; `evt_...` means a failed photo.

**Fixes** (`brain/pipeline.py`, `brain/look.py`):
- The timer is now 65 s. NV's vision gives up after 20 s, but one camera worker takes photos in turn, so the look photo can wait behind others. The answer normally comes from the photo itself: described, described by the detector, or failed.
- When the timer does fire, it stays quiet if any motion, doorbell or speech came in after the question. This is a counter in `_process`, so it can't scroll out of the log the way the reviewer found the first version could. The late photo is still dropped (no visitor greeting for it).
- `photo_failed` reads NV's `described_by` (#43). A "detector" description with source `camera` is spoken, not "I can't see". No description at all (source `camera`, "Photo saved.") is still a failure.

**Tests:** `brain/tests/test_look.py`: quiet after something else happened, the timeout is longer than vision, a detector description is spoken.
