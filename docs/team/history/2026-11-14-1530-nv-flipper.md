# Flipper driver (Step 1, NV)

- **Laptop / branch / PR:** NV, `nv/flipper`, PR link is in the PR list
- **What changed:**
  - New `hardware/flipper/` package: connection, parser, bursts and device code, plus `manual_test.py`, `capture.py`, tests and real samples.
  - New `hardware/settings.py`, a settings loader that picks keys per OS.
  - Nothing outside `hardware/` and this note.
- **How it was tested:** on real hardware (Flipper on COM5, Windows), plus 25 unit tests (`python -m unittest hardware.flipper.test_flipper`) that replay the real captures.
- **What works on real hardware:**
  - One doorbell hold gives one event (2 of 2 at 14:40).
  - One wave gives one motion event (17 packets, ~1.96 s, every time).
  - Outlet 1 switches on and off, and rx resumes ~1.2 s after each send. A motion event arrived 1.3 s after a send.
  - LED colours follow mode and alert, with the alert colour winning over the mode colour.
  - Alarm gives three buzz pulses.
  - Reconnects automatically after the cable is pulled.
- **What's broken or faked:**
  - **Doorbell reception is marginal.** Quick taps never decode. Holds went from 2 of 2 early on to about 1 in 3 later, and the same happened with raw `subghz rx` with no driver involved, so it's the radio, not the code. Next steps: fresh battery in the doorbell, check its real frequency with Sub-GHz → Frequency Analyzer on the Flipper, and keep the doorbell closer to the Flipper. Also note the doorbell sent key `…601` once (button nibble 1 instead of 8); the driver now matches on the serial.
  - **Firmware resets the LED** after `vibro` and after a few idle minutes. Worked around by re-sending the colour every 15 s and right after the buzz. You may still see a brief flicker during the alarm buzz.
  - `manual_test.py` hasn't been run interactively yet. The same driver calls were tested through a scripted session.
- **Next step:** `integration/` wires `Flipper.start()`, `on_event`, `execute()` and `apply_event()` into the bus. Mac mini check: VID/PID autodetect should find `/dev/cu.usbmodemflip_*` without any setting.
- **Proposals for config/PLAN (need agreement, not changed here):**
  - Purple is `r140 b255`.
  - Doorbell `min_duration_ms: 800` should be dropped, because a real hold decodes as a single packet with `duration_ms: 0`.
  - Optional new keys `flipper.burst_gap_s` (1.5) and `flipper.led_refresh_s` (15). The code already has these defaults.
  - The SD card files are named `Outlet1_on.sub` and so on. The lowercase names in the settings work because FAT is case-insensitive.
