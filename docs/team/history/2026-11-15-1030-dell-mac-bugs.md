# 2026-11-15 10:30 Dell: three bug fixes after the freeze (owner)

1. **Standing tasks on the Mac's models.** "Remind me to take out the trash when I get home"
   got "I can't keep that as a task" on the Mac. The 9B answers through the no-think prompt,
   so its tool call can come back as text, with close names ("remind", "arrive_home") or with
   no action at all.
   - `brain/tasks._call` now also reads calls written as text, in JSON or Qwen XML form.
   - `_tidy_args` maps close variants and fills in a missing action from the call's other
     fields. It does this for `remember` only.
   - Bench: 26 `k_` cases, including the owner's exact phrases. All pass on the local 4B
     with `--no-think raw` (the Mac 9B's path) and with chat.
2. **Heard & said shows both sides.** The card kept at most 3 entries and dropped the oldest
   until the rest fit, so a long reply always pushed the owner's line out.
   - Up to 8 entries now, in a scrolling list. Nothing is dropped.
   - Each line is labelled You, Visitor or Faraday, from the speech channel.
   - The latest thing said to Faraday stays in view with the answer below it.
   - The passphrase is redacted by the log, as before. Test: `dashboard/tests/test_heard_said.py`.
3. **No keyword arming.** "Actually cancel that leaving it by the side door, make it the main
   door" armed the house.
   - `strong()` no longer arms. The house arms only when the model picks arming, the words are
     plainly about leaving or bed (`clearly_arming`), and the smart model (9B) agrees.
   - `clearly_arming` returns nothing for "don't", cancel or change words, or doors, deliveries
     and tasks.
   - `fallback._LEAVING` no longer matches "leaving it …".
   - The owner's phrasings route to the task tool: "actually make that the main door", "change
     the delivery spot to the main door", "cancel the side door thing".
   - "Cancel …, make it X" changes the task, and "the side door thing" finds the task by its
     words.
   - Without a model, the house never arms by voice. The dashboard still arms it.
