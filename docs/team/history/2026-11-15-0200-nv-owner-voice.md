# NV: owner voice check, display only (2026-11-15 02:00)

Owner item 10 (stretch), time-boxed to an hour. Built on Windows with a fake `argmax-cli diarize`; the Mac measures how often it's right.

## How it works (`hardware/owner_voice.py`)

1. **Enroll once:** `python3 -m hardware.owner_voice enroll` records about 10 s of the owner's voice to `cache/owner_voice.wav` (gitignored, and `--fresh` leaves it alone).
2. **Each owner request** (after "Hey Faraday"): the enrollment, 0.5 s of silence and the request are joined into one wav, and `argmax-cli diarize` runs on it once (the same command as the visitor voice count, `stt.mac.diarize`).
   - With RTTM output (what argmax-cli prints), the check is per time range. If every voice in the request part is the enrollment's main speaker, it's `true`. If another voice speaks for 0.3 s or more, it's `false`.
   - With only a speaker count, it falls back to your rule: one voice = owner, two = unknown voice.
   - A failed diarization, or nothing heard, gives `null`.
3. **The result** goes on `speech_heard.owner_voice`. The check runs alongside STT and is waited for at most `audio.voice_check.grace_s` (2 s); a late result is only printed. The console prints `[voice] the owner's voice` or `[voice] unknown voice, not the enrolled owner (display only)`.

**Display only:** nothing is allowed or refused because of it. Visitor speech is never checked. Until someone enrolls, the check is off.

## Scoring it on the Mac

- `python3 -m hardware.owner_voice trial`: 10 short requests, each scored, then the total. Enforce for disarm only at 9/10 or better.
- Then run the trial once more with someone else speaking: run it with `--other`: they should get "unknown voice".
- `python3 -m hardware.owner_voice check some.wav` checks one recording.

## Review (fresh reviewer agent) and fixes

- **MEDIUM, fixed:** a one-breath "Hey Faraday, <request>" waited up to 2 s, because the check started only after the wake STT. Now it starts *alongside* the wake STT for segments long enough to hold a request. During a warning or alarm the app never waits for it, so "Hey Faraday, <passphrase>" disarms at once (`Voice.urgent`, from the alert level).
- **Diarize output:** `Diarizer.run()` returns (stdout, stderr) again, read stdout first, so log lines on stderr can't change a visitor's voice count.
- **Trial:** it counts owner, unknown voice and can't-tell separately, and `--other` scores a run with someone else speaking.
- **Small ones:** enrollment is moved with `shutil.move` (works across disks), the late-result race is closed with a lock, and the labels follow your words (false = "unknown voice").
- **Process:** this changes `shared/` (`speech_heard.owner_voice`) and `config/` (`audio.voice_check`). Both were asked for by you, documented for Dell in PLAN Q34 and FORMATS.md, and are display only.
